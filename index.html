About Thermo Guard

AI-Powered Extreme Heatwave Early Warning & Human Thermal Stress Index Platform for India

Mission

Thermo Guard protects lives by providing real-time heatwave early warnings and human thermal stress analysis. Built for Smart India Hackathon 2026 (Problem SIH26083), serving citizens and government disaster management authorities across India.

Scientific Methodology

Heat Index (HI)

Rothfusz Regression (NOAA/NWS, 1990)

9-term polynomial regression modeling perceived temperature from air temperature and relative humidity. Includes boundary adjustments for extreme humidity.

WBGT

Australian BoM Simplified (ISO 7243)

Wet Bulb Globe Temperature using the simplified outdoor approximation. The gold standard for occupational heat stress assessment.

UTCI

Bröde et al. (2012) Approximation

Universal Thermal Climate Index based on the Fiala multi-node thermoregulation model. Uses regression approximation.

HTSS Weighting

45%UTCI
35%WBGT
20%Heat Index

Non-compensatory safety guardrail: extreme danger on any single index cannot be masked by lower values in others.
