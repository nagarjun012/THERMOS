Why THERMOS? Fusing Environmental Variables into Human Thermal Safety

1. THE PROBLEM

Temperature alone is misleading. 40°C in dry air is manageable; 40°C at 75% humidity is deadly because body sweat cannot evaporate.

2. THERMOS SOLUTION

THERMOS calculates real-time UTCI, WBGT, and Heat Index to derive a single, non-compensatory Human Thermal Stress Score (HTSS).

3. ACTIONABLE OUTPUT

Instantly provides location-based risk ratings, root causes, and immediate hydration/work safety guidance for citizens and disaster authorities.

40°C Exposure Paradox: Same Temperature ≠ Same Heat Stress

Standard air temperature warnings fail because human heat stress depends on humidity, solar irradiance, and wind speed. Below is a real-time scientific comparison of 40°C under two different environmental conditions:

Scenario A: Dry Desert Heat HTSS: 48 / 100 (MODERATE)

Humidity: 18%

Sweat evaporates efficiently. Body thermoregulation functions with adequate shade & hydration.
Scenario B: Coastal Humid Heat HTSS: 94 / 100 (EXTREME DANGER)

Sweat evaporation is impossible. High risk of heat exhaustion without active shade, ORS hydration, and rest.

Data Sources & Methodology

THERMOS — Thermal Health Risk & Early-warning Management System (SIH 2026 Problem SIH26083)

Primary Data Sources

1. Open-Meteo Weather API

Provides real-time and 72-hour forecast hourly data for Temperature (2m), Relative Humidity, Wind Speed (10m), Direct Solar Irradiance, Dew Point, and Surface Pressure.

2. India Meteorological Department (IMD) Guidelines

Heatwave criteria for plains (>=40°C), coastal regions (>=37°C), and hilly areas (>=30°C) with departure thresholds.

3. NOAA / NWS Biometeorological Algorithms

Rothfusz 9-term polynomial Heat Index regression equations with low/high humidity boundary adjustments.

4. ISO 7243 & UTCI Fiala Model Standards

Wet Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT) simplified BoM model and Universal Thermal Climate Index (UTCI) multi-node thermoregulation model.

Scientific Thermal Models

Heat Index (HI)

Rothfusz Regression (NOAA)

Models perceived temperature combining air temp and relative humidity when T ≥ 26.7°C (80°F).

WBGT Index

ISO 7243 Simplified BoM

Gold standard for occupational outdoor heat stress incorporating vapor pressure and solar irradiance.

UTCI Index

Bröde et al. (Fiala Model)

Advanced biometeorological index representing dynamic human physiological heat balance.

HTSS Calculation Engine

45%UTCI Weight
35%WBGT Weight
20%Heat Index Weight

Rule-Based Non-Compensatory Guardrail: HTSS = max(weighted_sum, 0.85 * max(n_utci, n_wbgt, n_hi)). This ensures extreme danger on any single thermal metric is never hidden by moderate values in others.

System Limitations

  • API Dependency: Real-time accuracy relies on Open-Meteo weather API service availability and regional weather station network updates.
  • Spatial Resolution: Weather data is sampled per district/city coordinates (~1km-10km grid resolution). Microclimates within dense indoor alleys may vary.
  • Individual Physiological Variance: HTSS provides population-level environmental risk estimates. Individual heat tolerance varies based on age, pre-existing health conditions, hydration, and acclimation.
  • Operational Disclaimer: THERMOS is a public safety risk-warning platform and does not replace official emergency directives issued by the IMD or NDMA.
